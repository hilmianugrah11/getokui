01

Do I need a finished brand guide?

No. Aura can start with a screenshot, a rough product note, a color direction, or a DESIGN.md file. Better context gives better drafts, but you do not need a complete system before you begin.

02

Can Aura use screenshots or existing sites as references?

Yes. Visual references are often better than text prompts. Upload an image of a layout you like, and Aura will extract the structural hierarchy and apply your design system to that skeleton.

03

Can I export the page?

Every page generated is clean HTML styled with standard Tailwind CSS classes. You can export the raw code, publish to a custom domain, or sync directly to your repository.

04

What if the first generation misses?

Iteration is built-in. Select any component and prompt a change, adjust the global DESIGN.md to restyle the whole page instantly, or provide a new screenshot to pivot the layout.
