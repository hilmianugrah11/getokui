Principles / 08

The rules behind the restraint.

A few working principles guide every commission, from the first site walk to the final junction, so the finished space feels calm, resolved, and built to last.

01

Site First

We design from the ground and its history outward, never from a house style inward.

  • Orientation, light, and landscape set the first rules.
  • Existing constraints are treated as design material.
  • Nothing is added without belonging to the place.
02

Resolved to the Detail

Every junction is drawn, tested, and clarified so the building stays calm where it matters most.

  • Key junctions are resolved before site pressure begins.
  • Material transitions are drawn, not guessed.
  • Small decisions are given enough time to settle.
03

One Team Throughout

The people who shape the work at the beginning stay close through drawings, coordination, and construction.

  • Senior attention remains present across every stage.
  • Knowledge is carried forward instead of handed off.
  • Clients know who is responsible for each decision.
04

Built to Endure

Materials and details are chosen for how they age, weather, repair, and continue to feel settled over time.

  • Durability is considered before surface effect.
  • Maintenance is designed into the architecture.
  • The palette is reduced so the building can age quietly.

Questions / 10

Clear answers, before we begin.

Do you take on small or single-room projects?

Yes. Alongside whole buildings we take on focused interior and extension projects — the brief just needs to be clear and the ambition real. Scale matters far less to us than care.

How long does a project usually take?

A typical house runs fourteen to twenty months from first sketch to completion; larger or planning-sensitive schemes take longer. We set out a realistic programme during feasibility so there are no surprises.

Do you work outside Spain and Denmark?

Often. We run projects across Europe from our two studios — travelling for key site stages and coordinating the rest closely over calls, models, and shared drawings.

Can you work with our engineer and contractor?

Gladly. We coordinate closely with consultants and builders, and can recommend trusted teams where you don't already have them in place.

How do we start?

Send a short note about the site and the brief. We'll arrange a call, visit if it's close, and follow up with a clear proposal and fee. No obligation, no jargon.
