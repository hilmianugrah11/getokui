FAQ

Common questions.

What exactly does Nexus do?
Nexus is a network security platform that gives you real-time visibility into every device and connection in your infrastructure. It automatically detects threats like intrusion attempts, misconfigurations, and unusual behavior — then responds instantly by isolating affected segments. Think of it as a 24/7 security operations center that runs itself.
How long does setup take?
Most teams are fully operational in under 10 minutes. You install a lightweight agent on your servers (one command), Nexus discovers your network automatically, and you turn on protection. No hardware changes required, and there's zero downtime during setup.
Will it slow down my network?
No. The Nexus agent uses less than 1% CPU and minimal memory. It operates passively by analyzing network metadata rather than inspecting full packet payloads, so your applications won't see any performance impact.
What compliance standards does Nexus support?
Nexus generates audit-ready reports for SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, HIPAA, and GDPR out of the box. Enterprise customers can also configure custom compliance frameworks, including FedRAMP. Reports are available on-demand or can be scheduled for automatic delivery.
Can I try Nexus for free?
Yes. The Starter plan is completely free for up to 50 endpoints with no time limit. You can also start a 14-day free trial of the Professional plan — no credit card required. If you need to evaluate Enterprise features, contact our sales team for a guided demo.
