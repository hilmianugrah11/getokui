FAQ

AeroNet Core questions.

How quickly can we deploy a new region?

Most teams connect their first region in under fifteen minutes. Full production rollout depends on compliance review, node count, and traffic policy complexity.

Can AeroNet Core run on private infrastructure?

Yes. Nodes can run in your cloud accounts, private data centers, edge facilities, or hybrid environments with dedicated relay pools.

What happens when a relay degrades?

AeroNet Core scores node health continuously. When latency, packet loss, or availability drifts outside policy, sessions are moved to healthier paths automatically.

Does the platform support compliance boundaries?

Traffic can be restricted by region, provider, node label, customer segment, or custom policy. Sovereign plans include deeper residency workflows.
