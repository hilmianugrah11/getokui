La Maison

A legacy written in bubbles

"Champagne is not merely a drink -- it is a philosophy of living, a celebration of the patience required to turn sunlight and soil into something eternal."

Founded in 1842 by Auguste Aurelia in the chalky cellars beneath Epernay, Maison Aurelia has remained a family affair for six generations. We tend 62 hectares of Grand Cru vineyards across the Cote des Blancs and Montagne de Reims, practicing biodynamic viticulture and hand-harvesting every cluster.

Our Heritage

180 years beneath the chalk

Our cellars extend two kilometers beneath the hills of Epernay, carved into Cretaceous chalk 30 meters below the surface. Here, at a constant 12 degrees Celsius, millions of bottles undergo the slow, sacred transformation from wine to champagne.
