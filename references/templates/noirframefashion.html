Fashion questions / FAQ

The questions fashion teams ask before turning a collection into a campaign world.

What should we send
Send moodboards, campaign references, product shots, fabric details, brand notes, existing assets or a rough creative direction.
How fast can a campaign launch
A first direction can move in 72 hours. A polished campaign page, lookbook and content system usually takes 7 to 21 days.
Do we need a full shoot
Not always. We can work from existing product photography, campaign assets, references and AI-assisted visual systems when appropriate.
What makes a good brief
A clear audience, collection mood, product priority, references, launch date and the assets you already have.
