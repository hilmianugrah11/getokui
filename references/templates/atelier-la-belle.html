Quick Answers

FAQs.

What services does Atelier La Belle offer?

We specialize in brand identity, editorial web design, UI/UX, motion graphics, Shopify e-commerce experiences, and overall creative direction tailored specifically for fashion and beauty brands.

Do you work with small brands or startups?

Yes, we love working with emerging brands that have a clear vision and an appreciation for premium design. Our "Brand Launch" package is specifically designed to give startups a high-end foundation.

Can you redesign an existing Shopify store?

Absolutely. We frequently audit and redesign existing Shopify stores to improve aesthetics, user experience, and conversion rates, elevating them to meet luxury standards.

How long does a project usually take?

Depending on the scope, a standard branding and web design project takes between 6 to 10 weeks. We ensure every detail is refined before launch.
